About these terms

These terms apply to every service Axe Connect provides: software and web development, website maintenance, graphic design, IT support and computer repairs. By requesting a quote, accepting a quote, paying a deposit or handing over a device, you agree to these terms.

Where you buy as a consumer, nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008.

Quotes

  • Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date they are sent.
  • A quote covers only the scope described in it. Work outside that scope is quoted separately and only starts once you approve it.
  • Approval by WhatsApp or email counts as written acceptance.

Payment

  • Software, website and design projects require a 50% deposit before work starts. The balance is due on completion, before final handover.
  • IT support and repairs are paid on completion unless agreed otherwise.
  • Monthly plans are billed in advance at the start of each month.
  • Invoices are payable within 7 days. Work may be paused while an invoice is overdue.

Software, web & design projects

Your part

You provide content (text, images, logos), access details and feedback on time. Delays in receiving these move the delivery date by the same amount.

Revisions

Each project includes 2 rounds of revisions. Further rounds, or changes to the agreed scope, are charged separately.

Launch and bug fixes

After launch you get 30 days of free bug fixes for problems in the work we delivered. New features, content changes and issues caused by third-party changes are not bug fixes and are quoted separately or covered by a maintenance plan.

Third-party services

Domain names, hosting, email, paid plugins, app-store fees and stock images are paid by you and fall under those providers' own terms. We can set them up in your name so you stay the owner.

Handover

Final files, source code and logins are handed over once the invoice is paid in full.

Ownership & portfolio

  • Once paid in full, you own the final website, software and designs made specifically for you.
  • Axe Connect keeps ownership of its general tools, templates and code libraries used across projects, and gives you a permanent licence to use them as part of your project.
  • We may show finished work in our portfolio unless you ask us in writing not to.

Maintenance & monthly plans

  • Monthly website maintenance and business IT support plans cover what is listed in your plan.
  • Unused hours do not carry over to the next month.
  • Either side can cancel a monthly plan with 30 days' written notice.

IT support & repairs

Assessing a device or problem carries a diagnosis fee, which we confirm before we start.

The diagnosis fee is non-refundable. If you go ahead with the repair or service, the diagnosis fee is included in the final amount charged, so you don't pay it twice.

Back up your data

Please back up your data before handing over a device. We take care with your files, but Axe Connect is not responsible for data loss during repairs, reinstalls or virus removal. Data recovery is a separate service.

Parts

Part prices and availability can change. We confirm the price with you before ordering any part.

On-site and remote support

  • On-site visits may carry a call-out fee, which is quoted before the visit. Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel a booked visit.
  • Remote support sessions only take place with your permission, and you can end a session at any time.

Uncollected devices

We will remind you to collect a finished device. Devices not collected within 90 days of that notice may be sold or disposed of to recover the costs owed.

Warranty

  • New parts may also carry the manufacturer's own warranty.

Cancellations & refunds
