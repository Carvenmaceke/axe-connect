Who we are

Axe Connect is the responsible party for personal information collected through axeconnect.co.za and while providing our services. This policy explains how we handle your information under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you use our contact or quote forms, message us or book a service, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your business or project name, if you give one
  • Details of the project, device or problem you describe
  • Access details you choose to share so we can do the work, such as website or hosting logins
  • Your email address, if you subscribe to our newsletter

The website remembers your light/dark theme and service choice in your own browser. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and prepare quotes
  • To deliver, support and invoice the work you asked for
  • To keep records required by law, such as invoices

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we use your email address to send occasional news about sales, special prices and new services. We only do this with your consent, and every email tells you how to unsubscribe. To unsubscribe at any time, reply "unsubscribe" or email carvenmaceke9@gmail.com.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for any other marketing.

Who we share it with

Form messages are sent to us through EmailJS and delivered to our email inbox. We share information with other providers (for example hosting or domain companies, or parts suppliers) only when needed to do your work. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and project details for as long as needed to provide the service, and financial records for as long as the law requires. Login details you share for a job are deleted or should be changed once the job is done.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. When you share passwords with us, we recommend changing them after the work is finished.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us to:

  • Confirm what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy
  • Correct information that is wrong or out of date
  • Delete information we no longer need
  • Stop using your information, where the law allows

Email carvenmaceke9@gmail.com with your request. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
