Can I hire you as a freelance software developer?

Yes. Companies and individuals hire Axe Connect as a freelance or contract software developer, for a single project or ongoing work. Everything is done remotely, so location doesn't matter.

Do you build custom software and web applications?

Yes. Beyond websites, we build web applications, back-end systems, APIs and databases, such as booking systems, inventory tools, client portals and admin dashboards, using technologies like Java, Spring Boot, SQL and JavaScript.

Can I hire you as a freelance web developer if I'm not in Pretoria?

Yes. Web development and design are done fully remotely, so we work with individuals and companies anywhere in South Africa and abroad. We keep in touch by WhatsApp, email, phone or video call.

What kind of websites do you build?

Business and company websites, startup websites, landing pages, e-commerce and online stores, portfolio and personal websites, website redesigns and custom web applications.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and the features you need. Send a quote request with your budget, anything from under R1,500 to R10,000+, and we'll recommend what fits.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page can be ready quickly, and most small business websites are delivered within a few weeks. You get a clear timeline in your quote.

Will my website be mobile-friendly and SEO-ready?

Yes. Every site is built mobile-first, loads fast and is set up with the SEO basics (page titles, descriptions, headings and structured data) so people can find you on Google.

Do you work with companies and agencies on contract?

Yes. Companies can outsource web development projects, hire us for ongoing website maintenance and updates, or bring us in on contract for a specific build.

Can you set up my domain, hosting and business email?

Yes. We can register or connect your domain, set up hosting and configure business email so everything works together.

Do you also do logos and branding?

Yes. Logo design, posters and flyers, social media graphics, business cards and full brand identity packs, so your website and brand match.
