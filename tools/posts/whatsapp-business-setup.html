For many South African customers, WhatsApp is the first way they contact a business. If you are still running your business from a personal WhatsApp number, you are missing tools that save time and make you look far more professional. The free WhatsApp Business app fixes that in about half an hour.

WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business?

WhatsApp Business is a separate free app made for small businesses. It works like normal WhatsApp, but adds a business profile, a product catalogue, automatic greeting and away messages, quick replies and labels to organise chats.

You can keep your personal WhatsApp on the same phone if your phone supports two numbers (for example with a second SIM or eSIM). Many owners prefer a separate business number, so they can switch off after hours.

Step 1: Install and move your number

  1. Download WhatsApp Business from the Play Store or App Store.
  2. Enter the number you want to use for the business. If it is currently on normal WhatsApp, the app offers to move it, including your chat history.
  3. Choose your business name. Use your real trading name, exactly as it appears on your signage and Google profile.

Step 2: Complete your business profile

Go to Settings → Business tools → Business profile and fill in every field:

  • Profile photo: your logo, cropped so it fits a circle.
  • Category: the closest match to what you do.
  • Description: one or two sentences on what you offer and where. For example: "Plumbing repairs, geysers and drain cleaning in Johannesburg. PIRB registered."
  • Address (or service area), business hours, email and website.

Customers see this when they tap your name, so a complete profile builds trust before you even reply.

Step 3: Set up automatic messages

Greeting message

Sent automatically to new customers or anyone who has not messaged in 14 days. Use it to set expectations:

"Hi, thanks for contacting [Business]! Tell us what you need and your area, and we'll reply shortly. For a quote, you can also use our form at yourbusiness.co.za."

Away message

Sent outside your business hours, so customers are not left wondering:

"Thanks for your message! We're closed right now and open again at 8:00. We'll reply first thing."

Step 4: Save quick replies

Quick replies are saved answers you send by typing a shortcut. Under Business tools → Quick replies, save answers to the questions you get every day:

  • /prices: your price list or how quotes work
  • /bank: your banking details and reference instructions
  • /hours: opening times and address
  • /thanks: a thank-you message with your Google review link

Quick replies keep your answers consistent, avoid typos in banking details and save a surprising amount of typing.

Step 5: Add a catalogue

The catalogue is a mini shop window inside WhatsApp. Add your main products or services with a photo, price (or "from" price) and short description. When a customer asks what you offer, send them the catalogue instead of a long list of messages.

Step 6: Use labels to stay organised

Labels let you tag chats, for example New enquiry, Quote sent, Paid and Follow up. At the end of each day, check the Follow up label so no customer slips through the cracks.

Step 7: Make it easy to start a chat

WhatsApp gives every number a short link in the form wa.me/27XXXXXXXXX (your number in international format, without the 0). Put it on your website, Google Business Profile, Instagram bio and email signature. Under Business tools → Short link you can also create a link with a pre-written message, such as "Hi, I'd like a quote".

Good habits that win customers

  • Reply quickly, even if only to say you'll send the quote later today.
  • Send quotes as PDFs rather than typed messages, so they look professional and are easy to find later.
  • Never share card details over WhatsApp, and never ask customers to. Use a payment link instead.
  • Ask happy customers for a Google review once the job is done.
  • Back up your chats in the app's settings, so you do not lose your customer history with your phone.

Common questions

Can more than one person answer the business WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp Business lets you link the account to a few other devices, such as a computer using WhatsApp Web or desktop, so staff can reply from the office. For larger teams handling many chats, the paid WhatsApp Business Platform (API) through an approved provider is the next step.

Will customers see that I use WhatsApp Business?

They will see your business profile, with your logo, description and hours, and a label showing it is a business account. That helps customers trust that they are talking to the real business.

Can I send promotions to all my customers?

You can use broadcast lists, but they only reach people who have saved your number, and you should only message customers who are happy to hear from you. For regular promotions, an email list on your website gives you more control and lets people unsubscribe easily.
