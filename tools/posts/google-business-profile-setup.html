When someone searches "plumber near me" or "laptop repair Pretoria", the first thing Google often shows is a map with three businesses on it. Those listings come from Google Business Profiles. Setting one up is free, and for a local business it is one of the most valuable things you can do online. Here is how to do it properly.

Before you start

  • A Google account you will keep long-term. Use one the business controls, not a staff member's personal account.
  • Your business name exactly as customers know it, your main phone number and your website address if you have one.
  • A few good photos: your shopfront or workspace, your work, your team, and your logo.

Step 1: Create the profile

  1. Go to business.google.com and sign in.
  2. Enter your business name. If Google suggests an existing listing for your business, claim that instead of creating a duplicate.
  3. Choose your primary category carefully. It is one of the biggest factors in which searches you appear for. Pick the most specific one that fits, such as "Computer repair service" rather than "Service".

Step 2: Location or service area

This is where many people go wrong:

  • If customers come to you (a shop, salon or office), add your address.
  • If you go to customers or work remotely (plumbers, mobile mechanics, IT support, caterers), you can set a service area instead, such as Pretoria and surrounding suburbs, and hide your home address.

Step 3: Verify the business

Google needs to confirm your business is real before your profile shows publicly. The options depend on your business. Often you are asked to record a short video showing your location, signage, equipment or tools, and proof you manage the business. Some businesses are offered phone, email or postcard verification instead. Follow the method Google gives you; it is free.

Never pay anyone who contacts you claiming they need a fee to "verify" or "keep" your Google listing. Google does not charge for Business Profiles.

Step 4: Complete every section

Profiles with complete information look more trustworthy to customers. Add:

  • Hours, including special hours for public holidays
  • Phone number and website
  • Services with a short description of each
  • A clear business description explaining what you do and where
  • Photos: logo, cover photo, and real photos of your work

Keep the business name as your real name. Adding extra keywords like "Best Cheap Laptop Repairs Pretoria" to the name goes against Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.

Step 5: Get reviews and keep it active

Reviews matter to customers and to how Google ranks local businesses.

  • In your profile, find the Ask for reviews option. It gives you a short link you can send on WhatsApp after every finished job.
  • Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly. Customers read the replies too.
  • Add new photos and use Posts to share offers, new services or recent work.
  • Keep your hours and phone number up to date, especially over holidays.

Common problems

  • Verification keeps failing: make sure your name, address or service area and phone number match what is on your signage, website and other listings.
  • Profile suspended: usually caused by a name with extra keywords, a virtual office address, or a service-area business showing a home address. Fix the issue and request reinstatement from the profile.
  • Not showing in searches: new profiles take time. Complete every section, add photos and start collecting reviews.
