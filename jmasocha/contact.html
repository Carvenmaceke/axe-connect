FAQ

Good to know

How do I book a shoot?

Fill in the form on this page or message us on WhatsApp at 066 132 2462. We’ll reply with packages, prices and available dates.

Where is the studio?

We’re at Shop 5A, Centre Walk, 266 Pretorius Street, Pretoria Central. We also shoot outdoors at locations around Pretoria.

Can I bring my own theme or concept?

Yes. Choose “Custom request” and describe your idea — colours, props, outfits or inspiration photos — and we’ll plan the set with you.

Do you do video as well?

Yes. We film events, graduations, outdoor stories and corporate videos. Ask for a combined photo and video package.

Can you print photos I took myself?

Yes. Send us your photos on WhatsApp and we’ll print them on canvas, frames, mugs, tumblers, globes, plates or T-shirts.
