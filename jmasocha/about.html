About us

Pretoria’s milestone studio since 2010

J Masocha Photography is a photography, videography and printing studio in the heart of Pretoria Central.

Who we are

Your story, beautifully told.

Since 2010 we’ve photographed graduations, first birthdays, pregnancies, love stories and businesses across Pretoria. We combine creative direction, attention to detail and a welcoming studio experience to deliver more than photos — memories you’ll want to print and keep.
